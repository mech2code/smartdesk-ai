# Password Reset Guide

## Overview
This guide covers how to reset your corporate account password. Passwords expire every 90 days and must meet complexity requirements.

## Self-Service Reset (Recommended)

1. Go to `accounts.company.com/reset` from any browser (no VPN required).
2. Enter your corporate email address (e.g., `jdoe@company.com`).
3. Click **Send Reset Link**.
4. Check your personal or recovery email for a message from `noreply@company.com`.
5. Click the link in the email (valid for 30 minutes).
6. Enter and confirm your new password.
7. Click **Save**. Your password is updated immediately across all systems.

## Password Requirements
- Minimum 12 characters
- At least one uppercase letter (A–Z)
- At least one lowercase letter (a–z)
- At least one number (0–9)
- At least one special character (!@#$%^&*)
- Cannot reuse the last 10 passwords
- Cannot contain your name or username

## Locked Account

If your account is locked after too many failed attempts:
1. Wait 15 minutes — the lock clears automatically.
2. Use the self-service reset at `accounts.company.com/reset`.
3. If still locked, contact IT Support with your employee ID.

## Forgotten Username

Your corporate username is your first initial + last name (e.g., John Doe → `jdoe`).
If you cannot determine your username, email `it-support@company.com` from your personal email.

## Changing Your Password Proactively

1. Press **Ctrl+Alt+Del** (Windows) or go to **System Preferences → Users & Groups** (macOS).
2. Select **Change Password**.
3. Enter your current password, then the new password twice.
4. Click OK.

## After a Reset

- Update saved passwords in your browser.
- Re-authenticate on mobile devices (Outlook, Teams, etc.).
- Re-authenticate on VPN (Cisco AnyConnect).
- You will be signed out of all active sessions automatically.

## Troubleshooting

### "Reset link not received"
- Check spam/junk folder.
- Ensure you used the correct recovery email on file.
- Links expire after 30 minutes — request a new one.

### "Password does not meet requirements"
- Review requirements above and try a passphrase (e.g., `Blue$Sky#Mountains99`).

### "Account still locked after reset"
- Submit a helpdesk ticket or call IT Support: ext. 4357 (HELP).
