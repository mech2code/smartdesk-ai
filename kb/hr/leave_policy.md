# HR Policy — Leave Policy

## What happens if I forget to apply for compensatory off in the leave management system after getting email approval?

If you forget to apply for compensatory off in the leave management system after getting email approval, your leave will not be officially recorded or approved. It is essential to complete the process in the leave management system to ensure your compensatory off is recognized and can be availed.

## Can I avail compensatory off before it is approved in the leave management system?

No, you must wait for your compensatory off to be approved in the company’s leave management system before availing it. This ensures that your leave is properly documented and authorized.

## Is it possible to combine compensatory off with other types of leave?

Yes, you can combine compensatory off with other types of leave, but it cannot be clubbed with more than two types of leave at a time, such as casual leave or earned leave.

## Do public holidays have a different overtime calculation?

Yes, overtime for work performed on public holidays is calculated separately and requires prior approval, according to company policy.

## Do I need approval to work overtime on weekends or public holidays?

Yes, working overtime on weekends or public holidays requires prior approval according to company policy.

## What is the process for informing about planned and unplanned leaves in our company?

For planned leaves, you must inform the 'Leave Plans' channel at least a week in advance and obtain approval from your manager to ensure proper workload distribution. For unplanned sick leave, update the 'Leave Plans' channel between 9:30 to 10 AM or before your scrum, whichever is earlier.

## If I am taking leave for more than two days, is there anything specific I need to do?

Yes, if you are taking leave for two or more days, you should schedule an out-of-office message, such as 'Unavailable as I will be traveling,' to inform your colleagues of your extended absence.

## What are the guidelines for leave reporting and availability updates in a remote work policy?

Employees are required to inform their reporting manager and update the HR portal in advance for any planned leave or absence. In the case of unplanned leave, such as a medical emergency, employees should notify their manager as soon as possible via call or message. Additionally, employees are expected to update their status on internal communication tools like Slack or Teams if they are temporarily unavailable during working hours. Regular absenteeism or failure to report leave may affect eligibility for work-from-home or hybrid arrangements.

## What should an employee do if they need to take an unplanned leave due to a medical emergency?

In the event of an unplanned leave, such as a medical emergency, the employee should notify their manager as soon as possible, preferably via call or message. This ensures that the manager is aware of the absence and can make necessary arrangements.
