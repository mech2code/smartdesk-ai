# HR Policy — Remote Work

## What are the communication and collaboration expectations for remote employees?

Remote employees are expected to be reachable via phone, email, and internal chat during working hours. Attendance at scheduled meetings, whether virtual or in-person, is mandatory unless excused by the manager. Employees should proactively communicate their progress, challenges, and support needs to their team and manager. All work-related communication and document sharing should be conducted using approved collaboration tools such as Google Workspace, Slack, or Zoom.

## How is employee performance monitored and reviewed in a remote or hybrid work setting?

Managers set clear deliverables and key performance indicators (KPIs) for each employee. Regular check-ins and performance reviews are conducted to assess output and address any concerns. Consistent underperformance or lack of communication may lead to a review of the employee's eligibility for work-from-home or hybrid arrangements.

## Are there any restrictions on device usage for accessing company data while working remotely?

Yes, employees are required to use company-provided devices for all official work. Personal devices should not be used to access sensitive company data to ensure data security and compliance with company policies.

## How are exceptions to the remote work policy handled?

Any exceptions to the remote work policy guidelines require written approval from management. The policy is reviewed periodically and updated as needed to address changing requirements or circumstances.

## What are the consequences of violating the WFH policy at Kreeda Labs?

At Kreeda Labs, violating the Work From Home (WFH) policy can result in a series of escalating consequences. The first instance of non-compliance may lead to a verbal or written warning. If violations continue, the second instance can result in a temporary suspension of WFH privileges. A third violation may lead to the permanent revocation of WFH privileges, requiring the employee to work from the office. Examples of policy violations include missing meetings without prior notice, being repeatedly unresponsive during working hours, frequent delays in completing tasks, or unauthorized absences.

## What actions are considered violations of the WFH policy at Kreeda Labs?

Violations of the WFH policy at Kreeda Labs include failure to attend meetings without prior intimation, repeated unresponsiveness during working hours, frequent delays in task completion or missing deadlines, and unauthorized absences or misuse of WFH privileges. These actions can lead to disciplinary measures as outlined in the company's policy.

## How does Kreeda Labs support employee well-being and work-life balance in its WFH policy?

Kreeda Labs encourages employees to take short breaks to maintain productivity and avoid burnout. The company advises managing workloads effectively and reaching out to HR for mental health support or guidance if needed. These measures are designed to promote a healthy work-life balance while working remotely.

## What should I do if I feel overwhelmed or stressed while working remotely at Kreeda Labs?

If you feel overwhelmed or stressed while working remotely at Kreeda Labs, you are encouraged to take short breaks to help maintain productivity and prevent burnout. Additionally, you should reach out to the HR department for mental health support or guidance. The company prioritizes employee well-being and offers resources to help manage stress.

## What is the overall approach of Kreeda Labs towards remote work?

Kreeda Labs emphasizes trust, flexibility, and accountability in its approach to remote work. The company aims to create a productive, secure, and balanced WFH or hybrid environment. Employees are encouraged to adhere to guidelines, communicate effectively, and maintain a healthy work-life balance for a seamless remote working experience.

## What happens if I consistently underperform while working remotely?

If you consistently underperform while working remotely, it may lead to disciplinary action. This could include the revocation of your work-from-home privileges, depending on the severity and frequency of the underperformance. Regular feedback and support will be provided to help you improve before any such measures are taken.

## How often will performance reviews be conducted for remote employees?

Performance reviews for remote employees will be conducted regularly. The specific frequency may vary depending on company policy, but the intent is to ensure ongoing productivity and alignment with organizational goals.

## What kind of disciplinary actions can be taken if I violate WFH guidelines?

If you violate work-from-home guidelines, disciplinary actions may include warnings, additional training, or in more serious cases, the revocation of your WFH privileges. The exact action will depend on the nature and frequency of the violation.
