# HR Policy — Benefits

## What are the benefits of Kreeda Labs' work-life balance policy for employees?

Kreeda Labs' work-life balance policy benefits employees by reducing stress, preventing burnout, and promoting overall well-being. By encouraging efficient task management within regular hours and fairly acknowledging extra work, the company fosters a positive and productive workplace.

## How does following the Anti-Bribery & Corruption Policy benefit Kreeda Labs?

By following the Anti-Bribery & Corruption Policy, Kreeda Labs ensures compliance with legal standards, protects its reputation, and fosters trust among employees, clients, and partners.

## Can you explain how ethical business practices benefit Kreeda Labs' stakeholders?

Ethical business practices benefit Kreeda Labs' stakeholders by fostering trust, reducing risks, and enhancing the company's reputation. This leads to stronger partnerships, increased loyalty, and long-term success for both the company and its stakeholders.

## What are the benefits of maintaining a high-performing workplace?

Maintaining a high-performing workplace leads to increased productivity, higher employee satisfaction, better collaboration, and improved business outcomes. It also helps attract and retain top talent, contributing to the long-term success of the organization.

## What are the benefits of exceeding client expectations in business relationships?

Exceeding client expectations can lead to increased client loyalty, positive word-of-mouth referrals, and a stronger reputation in the market. Satisfied clients are more likely to return for future business and recommend the company to others, which can drive growth and long-term success.

## How does responsible resource management benefit a company?

Responsible resource management benefits a company by reducing costs, improving efficiency, and minimizing waste. It also enhances the company's reputation, attracts environmentally conscious customers, and helps ensure long-term sustainability by preserving resources for future use.

## If I accidentally damage company equipment before returning it, what should I do?

If you accidentally damage company equipment, you should promptly inform your employer. The company may assess the damage and determine if repair or replacement costs will be deducted from your final settlement, as per company policy.

## If I accidentally send a confidential file to the wrong person, what should I do?

If you accidentally send a confidential file to the wrong person, you should immediately report the incident to your supervisor or the appropriate department, such as IT or compliance. Prompt reporting can help mitigate potential risks and demonstrates your commitment to following company policies.

## How does a Zero Tolerance Policy benefit employees?

A Zero Tolerance Policy benefits employees by creating a secure and respectful work environment. It helps prevent harmful behaviors, ensures that everyone is treated fairly, and promotes a culture of accountability and trust within the organization.

## How does regular POSH training benefit Kreeda Labs?

Regular POSH training benefits Kreeda Labs by promoting a culture of respect and safety, reducing the risk of incidents, ensuring legal compliance, and enhancing overall workplace morale and trust.

## How does following the Code of Conduct benefit employees at Kreeda Labs?

Following the Code of Conduct benefits employees at Kreeda Labs by promoting safety, ethical behavior, and productivity. It helps prevent conflicts, fosters mutual respect, and creates a supportive environment where everyone can thrive.
